Batanes is the northernmost province of the Philippines and part of the Cagayan Valley region. Use the cards below as an interactive field guide while you build your trip.

BACKGROUND & HERITAGE

Life built around wind, stone and sea.

Traditional Ivatan stone houses are among Batanes' most recognizable cultural features. Farming, fishing, food traditions and settlement patterns reflect generations of adaptation to an exposed island environment.

Come for the landscape. Stay curious about the people who shaped it.
